# Skywall

**Play: https://ltsiaras.github.io/Skywall/**

A free two-task driving game. With one hand you keep the car inside the box on a winding, hilly road. With the other you answer one kind of sign and let every other sign go, at the same time. Each run lasts three minutes, and both tasks adapt after every run to keep you at about 80%.

In homage to NeuroRacer, the game from the UCSF multitasking study. Skywall rebuilds its task from the published methods: Anguera et al., *Video game training enhances cognitive control in older adults*, *Nature* 501:97–101 (2013). Not affiliated with UCSF, Neuroscape or Akili.

**Controls:** arrow keys or WASD drive and Space answers. On a gamepad, the left stick drives and A or the right trigger answers. On a phone, hold the left half to steer and tap the right half to answer.

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no build step. Your levels are saved in your own browser.
