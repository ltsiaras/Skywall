    Stop 1 of 12 · 0 of 20 runs

    1. Look at the cross in the middle of the screen the whole time.
    2. Drive with your left hand. Arrow keys or WASD: left and right to steer, up to speed up, down to slow down. Keep the car inside the box: the yellow lines are the road, the red lines are the speed limits. Turns push you sideways; hills slow you down or speed you up. If you touch the edge of the box, the cross shakes.
    3. Answer with your right hand. When the green circle appears, press Space as fast as you can. Leave every other sign alone.
    4. A run lasts three minutes. After each run, both tasks adjust so you stay at about 80% on each. Get both above 80% to go up a level overall.

    Gamepad: left stick drives, A or the right trigger answers. Phone: hold the left half to steer, tap the right half to answer.
